# Security Policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `security@tavonel.com`. Do not open a public issue, include customer document contents, or access data beyond the minimum needed to demonstrate the issue.

Include the affected URL or component, reproducible steps, impact, and a safe way to contact you. TAVONEL will acknowledge a credible report within two business days, prioritize containment, preserve evidence, and provide status updates until remediation or a documented risk decision.

Only the current production deployment at `https://tavonel.com` is in scope. Social engineering, denial of service, automated high-volume scanning, physical attacks, third-party provider infrastructure, and accessing another tenant's data are out of scope. Good-faith testing must stop immediately if unexpected personal or customer data appears.
