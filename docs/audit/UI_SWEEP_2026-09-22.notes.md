## What the sweep did not find

Zero overlap, zero clipped text, zero horizontal overflow, zero contrast failures and zero
layout-shifting media, across 58 routes at 360 · 390 · 430 · 768 · 1280 · 1536, in Chromium and
WebKit, with reduced motion on and off. That is a result, not an empty run: the first passes of
this probe reported 1,461 overlaps and 22 contrast failures, and every one of them turned out to
be the probe measuring something a reader never sees — a range rectangle taller than its line
box, a `-webkit-line-clamp`'s cut-off line, a `display: table-cell` header painted under a
stacked mobile card, a header's contrast read against the dark `body` it floats over rather than
the cream panel actually behind it. Each exclusion is named in `scripts/qa/live-sweep.mjs` with
the case that produced it, and the count above is what survived them.

Two runs exist only to prove a negative and did: `--scheme light` returns
`rgb(9, 13, 20)` for `body` on every route, identical to dark — the site paints every colour from
tokens and declares no `prefers-color-scheme` block — and `--motion no-preference` returns the
same ledger as reduced motion, so no defect is hiding behind an animation.

What remains is the 44px touch floor and the 12px type floor, in places the rules that state them
could not reach.
